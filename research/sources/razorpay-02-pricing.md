What are payment processing charges?

### What are payment processing charges?

Payment gateway charges refer to the cost which is charged to facilitate online payment via the payment gateway (ex: Razorpay) on the website or app.

Payment gateway charges are calculated as a percentage of an online payment amount and vary according to the type of online payment methods used by the customer.

Are the payment gateway charges deducted on a per transaction basis?

### Are the payment gateway charges deducted on a per transaction basis?

Yes. The transaction fee and applicable GST are automatically deducted from the customer's payment at the source. The remaining amount is settled directly to your registered bank account according to your settlement cycle (typically T+1 or Instant).

Is there a transaction fee for standard UPI payments on Razorpay Payment Gateway?

### Is there a transaction fee for standard UPI payments on Razorpay Payment Gateway?

No, there is Zero MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) on standard bank-to-bank UPI transactions. This means when a customer pays you directly from their bank account using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.), merchants are not charged a transaction fee by the bank. However, a standard platform or technology fee of 2% will be applied which is for the dashboard, reporting tools, and the secure infrastructure provided by the Payment Gateway.

### Will I be charged if my customer uses a RuPay Debit Card?

No. RuPay Debit Card transactions are also covered under the Zero MDR policy. You will not incur any Merchant Discount Rate for these transactions.
