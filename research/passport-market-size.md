## Key Facts

- **5,77,03,550 Udyam registrations (including the report’s Udyam/UAP registration universe) as of 31 Dec 2024**: 5,69,01,755 micro, 7,32,782 small and 69,013 medium. This is the strongest broad-market denominator, but it is not the number of active, digitally enabled or export-ready businesses. Source: Ministry of MSME, *Annual Report 2024-25*, published 2025, data as of 31 Dec 2024: https://msme.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/06/cdd1fa9e3553498f59e6fef26bcc34b4.pdf (older than 18 months only if evaluated after June 2026; the underlying count is already >18 months old as of 2026).
- **Turnover distribution is available for sizing/down-funnel qualification** in the same report. As of 31 Dec 2024: <₹10 lakh: 2,22,39,239; ₹10–25 lakh: 48,81,454; ₹25–50 lakh: 21,76,323; ₹50 lakh–1 crore: 9,99,553; ₹1–2 crore: 7,18,493; ₹2–5 crore: 7,02,779; ₹5–25 crore: 5,81,235; ₹25–50 crore: 86,609; ₹50–100 crore: 40,775; ₹100–250 crore: 21,290. These are registration/declared-turnover bands, not validated GMV or export propensity. Same URL above.
- **Commerce Ministry’s 2024-25 Annual Report supports an e-commerce-export opportunity but does not establish a national count of e-commerce exporters.** It records the proposed E-Commerce Export Hubs (ECEHs), intended to help MSMEs/artisans access global markets, with warehousing, packaging, labelling, certification, logistics and returns infrastructure. It also says courier/postal exports can be made up to ₹10 lakh per consignment and that RoDTEP/RoSCTL/drawback incentives extend to courier exports. Source (published 2025): https://www.commerce.gov.in/files/2025-08/Commerce_AR-2024-25-English-1.pdf
- **The same Commerce report says approximately 62% of Indian SMEs use domestic e-commerce platforms**, but this is an e-commerce adoption claim, not cross-border participation; it needs original-study attribution and validation before using as a funnel conversion rate. Source: https://www.commerce.gov.in/files/2025-08/Commerce_AR-2024-25-English-1.pdf
- **No authoritative count of Indian IEC holders/exporters was located in the reviewed sources.** DGFT’s portal exposes “Number of IEC issued” as a dashboard metric, but the search result did not provide a stable count/date. Do not substitute FIEO’s “over 200,000 exporters” statement for an official exporter count: the Commerce report describes FIEO as serving the interests of over 200,000 exporters, which is an association/service-reach claim, not necessarily unique active exporters. DGFT: https://dgft.gov.in/CP ; Commerce annual report: https://www.commerce.gov.in/files/2025-08/Commerce_AR-2024-25-English-1.pdf
- **D2C count is directional and source-dependent, not ground truth.** BusinessLine reported in 2025 that India has “over 11,000 companies” and cited a sector report; an Economic Times report (2024) cited a different D2C estimate and projected market size. These should be treated as an external sector-report range, not an official census. BusinessLine: https://epaper.thehindubusinessline.com/ccidist-ws/bl/bl_delhi/issues/128273/OPS/GN6E8NFJP.1+G3JE8PRE7.1.html ; ET: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/indian-d2c-brand-market-poised-to-hit-61-3-billion-by-fy27/articleshow/110880609.cms

### Defensible Rize-fit funnel

1. **Broad formalized universe:** 5.77 crore Udyam/UAP registrations.
2. **Commercially meaningful subset:** Use declared turnover as a transparent qualification lens rather than inventing a percentage. The report shows ~10.99 lakh registrations in the ₹1 crore+ bands listed through ₹250 crore (sum of the listed ₹1 crore+ bands), but this is a *registration count derived from the report’s bands*, not a market forecast or active-merchant count.
3. **Digital-selling subset:** Intersect with businesses already using e-commerce or other digital channels. The Commerce report’s ~62% SME domestic-e-commerce-use claim can be shown only as a cited external indicator, not applied mechanically to the Udyam denominator.
4. **Export-curious subset:** Identify merchants showing observable intent—IEC application/holder status, export enquiries, international payments, courier/postal export usage, export-market catalogue, or participation in ECEH/market-access programmes. No official source found supports a universal conversion rate from MSMEs to this stage.
5. **Rize-fit pilotable subset:** Further require physical-goods D2C/e-commerce activity, repeatable SKU/fulfilment capability, willingness to provide product/material/claims evidence, and a consented transaction signal. This is a product-defined serviceable market, not a claimed national count.

## Claims requiring cross-reference

- “India has X exporters/IEC holders”: obtain a dated DGFT dashboard export or written DGFT/Commerce confirmation; do not infer from FIEO membership/service reach.
- “X e-commerce exporters” or “X cross-border sellers”: the reviewed government reports describe infrastructure/policy, not a national seller census. Cross-reference India Post/Dak Ghar Niryat Kendra, CBIC/courier export, DGFT, ONDC or marketplace administrative data.
- “62% of SMEs use e-commerce”: locate the underlying survey, sampling frame, year and definition of SME; it cannot be treated as cross-border adoption.
- “11,000 D2C brands”: verify the underlying 2024/25 sector report, entity definition and deduplication; BusinessLine is secondary reporting.
- Any implied market revenue, CAGR, TAM/SAM/SOM or conversion percentage: no consultant forecast should be presented as observed fact.

## Source quality

- **Tier 1:** Ministry of MSME Annual Report 2024-25; Department of Commerce Annual Report 2024-25; DGFT official portal. Government sources, but the MSME stock is as-of Dec 2024 and the Commerce report gives policy/program facts rather than seller counts.
- **Tier 2:** BusinessLine and Economic Times reporting on D2C sector reports. Useful directional triangulation; not official censuses and definitions differ.
- **Not usable as evidence of counts:** the fetched PIB PDF at https://www.commerce.gov.in/files/d-m-y/PIB2149736.pdf (dated 29 Jul 2025) did not contain the requested exporter/e-commerce/MSME statistics.

## Gaps

- Dated, deduplicated active IEC/exporter count.
- Count of MSMEs that exported in the latest year, with product/turnover split.
- Count and GMV/value of Indian e-commerce/courier/postal exporters, especially MSMEs.
- Count of active Indian D2C physical-goods brands and the subset already selling internationally.
- Reliable digital adoption and export-intent rates linked specifically to Udyam registrations.
- Product-category, geography, and platform splits needed to estimate a serviceable Rize pilot cohort.
