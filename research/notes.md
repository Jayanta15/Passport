## Findings Log

- India exported $778.13bn in FY2023-24; Apr–Dec 2024 reached an estimated $602.64bn, up 6.03%. [@commerce-ar]
- MSMEs contribute around 30% of GDP and over 45% of exports; 5.77 crore Udyam/UAP registrations were recorded at 31 Dec 2024. [@msme-ar]
- Passport differentiates upstream of payment collection: demand diagnosis, readiness, route, SKU-level requirements and pricing.
- Directional serviceable market: 11,000–33,000 merchants, explicitly based on an assumed 1%–3% of the ₹1 crore-plus registration pool.
- Base commercial scenario: 1,000 activated merchants, ₹150 crore enabled GMV, ₹4.5 crore illustrative processing revenue at 3%, and ₹75 lakh incremental Passport economics at 0.5%.
