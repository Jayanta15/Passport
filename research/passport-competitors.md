## Key Facts
- **Skydo — positioning/collections:** Skydo markets itself to Indian exporters, freelancers and businesses for inbound B2B payments; its official page says payments from 150+ countries, local receiving bank accounts, 24-hour processing, tracking, instant FIRA, and zero-forex-markup/flat transaction fees ($19 <=$2k; $29 $2,001-$10k; 0.3% >$10k). These are company claims, not independent validation. URL: https://www.skydo.com/features/receive-cross-border-payments (fetched copy saved; page undated).
- **Skydo — India status/compliance:** Official marketing page claims RBI PA-CB authorization and RBI/FEMA compliance. Search surfaced Economic Times reporting in-principle RBI authorization (Jan 2025 article URL: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/skydo-secures-rbi-licence-to-offer-cross-border-payments/articleshow/117413507.cms) and Times of India reporting a final authorization (search result dated Jan 2026; URL: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/skydo-receives-rbi-final-nod-for-cross-border-payments/articleshow/126431754.cms). Because the latter is future-dated relative to many current contexts and I did not retrieve an RBI authorization-list entry, treat final-status claim as requiring verification.
- **Skydo — accounts/FX/payments:** Public materials describe receiving accounts and inbound settlement, not a general multi-currency operating account or broad outbound-payments product. The reviewed public materials emphasize zero FX markup rather than a destination-specific rate sheet. URL: https://www.skydo.com/features/international-accounts (search result; company source).
- **Stripe — India positioning:** Stripe’s India global-availability page labels India “Preview” and directs businesses to contact sales; fetched page does not describe a general India account/FX product. URL: https://stripe.com/in/global (fetched copy saved; undated).
- **Stripe — inbound international collections:** Stripe’s India documentation says Indian accounts can accept international payments in 135+ currencies, subject to an active India account, valid IEC and the “Accepting International Transactions” process. URL: https://support.stripe.com/questions/accepting-international-payments-from-stripe-accounts-in-india (official support; publication date not visible in search result). Stripe docs also require additional fields for non-India-issued cards for regulatory reporting. URL: https://docs.stripe.com/india-accept-international-payments?locale=en-GB.
- **Stripe — compliance/onboarding:** Stripe support says it has worked with RBI to validate cross-border payment services and that India verification/KYC requirements apply; failure to complete KYC can restrict an account. URLs: https://support.stripe.com/questions/cross-border-payment-services-in-india and https://support.stripe.com/questions/2025-updates-to-india-verification-requirements.
- **Stripe — pricing/accounts/FX:** Public India pricing lists 2% for India-issued cards and 3% for cards issued outside India (company pricing; no page publication date found). URL: https://stripe.com/in/pricing. Public materials reviewed describe payment processing rather than an India multi-currency deposit account or merchant-specific destination-price recommendation.
- **Airwallex — positioning:** Official global product pages market Global Accounts, multi-currency collections, local payment methods, FX/transfers and payouts, including 160+ local payment methods and local-currency receiving. URLs: https://www.airwallex.com/en-uk/ppc/cross-border-payments and https://www.airwallex.com/en-fr/business-account/global-accounts. These pages are not India-specific and are promotional evidence only.
- **Airwallex — India availability:** Searches did not find an authoritative India product/launch/licence page. Airwallex’s public country/regulatory materials returned markets such as US, UK, Singapore, Australia, etc., but not clear India availability. Therefore: **India availability, India-local collection accounts, and India-specific outbound capability: not found in public materials reviewed**—not a finding that they do not exist. URL for general newsroom: https://airwallex.com/newsroom.
- **Regulatory baseline:** RBI’s PA-CB notification requires authorization for non-bank PA-CBs, FIU-IND registration prerequisite, merchant CDD/risk management, restrictions on prohibited goods, and export/import collection-account and settlement controls; it sets a ₹25 lakh per-unit goods/services transaction ceiling in the cited notification. URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12561 (official RBI; notification page date should be checked directly; fetched copy saved).

## Claims requiring cross-reference
1. Skydo’s RBI PA-CB status: reconcile Skydo’s own claim, Jan-2025 ET in-principle report, and Jan-2026 TOI final-authorisation report against RBI’s current authorized-entity list/letter. Do not present final approval as settled without primary RBI evidence.
2. Skydo’s 150+ countries, 24-hour settlement, zero FX markup, fee schedule, account availability, FIRA and customer/volume figures are provider claims; test against current terms and actual corridor limits.
3. Stripe “135+ currencies,” India international-payment eligibility, and Preview status may vary by account type/use case and change; confirm with current Stripe India sales/support and IEC flow.
4. Airwallex global product pages cannot establish India availability, Indian regulatory licensing, local collection accounts, or outbound corridors.
5. RBI notification transaction ceiling and compliance requirements are regulatory baseline, not proof that any named provider satisfies every requirement.
6. No public evidence reviewed supports the requested intelligence/diagnosis/licence-repository/route-liability-recommendation feature set; this is a bounded “not found,” not proof of nonexistence.
